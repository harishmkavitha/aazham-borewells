Mobile navigation V4 verification

Root cause fixed:
- The body-level backdrop had a z-index above the .site-nav stacking context, so it visually dimmed and intercepted clicks over the drawer.
- Floating actions were also in a separate stacking layer.

Fix:
- Raise .site-nav stacking context to z-index 10000 only while drawer is open.
- Backdrop uses z-index 9998 and sits below the entire nav.
- Drawer uses z-index 10001 inside the raised nav context.
- Removed backdrop-filter blur entirely.
- Floating theme/call/WhatsApp controls are non-interactive while drawer is open and remain below the backdrop.
- Close control relabeled clearly as Close menu.
